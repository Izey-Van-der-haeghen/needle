# Product

## Users

- **SEO specialists** who want depth: raw data, exports, competitor and SERP checks, one step away.
- **Marketers, content writers and website owners** with little SEO knowledge, who need plain-language verdicts and concrete fixes.
- **Developers** who hand-build pages and want a quick check before release.

Needle is for a broad, international audience. The interface is English; the checks work on pages in any language.

## Purpose

One Chrome extension that replaces a stack of single-purpose SEO extensions. Open it on any page and see what is wrong, why it matters and how to fix it, with the full data one step away. It succeeds when a non-specialist can find and explain a page's problems without help, and a specialist rarely needs another extension.

## Principles

1. **Verdict first, depth on demand.** Every check says what is wrong, why and how to fix it, in plain language.
2. **Accurate or explicit.** A check that cannot be measured reliably says so instead of guessing.
3. **Private by default.** No access to websites until a feature needs it; nothing leaves the browser unless the user starts a check that says so; no server, no analytics.
4. **Never spend money silently.** Paid DataForSEO lookups run only on a click, show their approximate cost first, and are cached.
5. **Useful without an account.** Every on-page, technical and AI-readiness check works with no login or payment. PageSpeed needs a free Google key; DataForSEO is optional.
6. **Ready to hand off.** Findings can be copied or exported in a form developers and writers can act on.

## Brand

- Name: **Needle**; store title "Needle: all-in-one SEO & GEO tool". The description also names the other terms people use for AI search optimisation (AEO, LLMO, AI SEO), in a sentence, never as a keyword list: the store forbids keyword spam.
- Izey's house style: paper, ink and one coral accent; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for text, Martian Mono only for the brand and small labels. Recognisable, never at the cost of readability.

## Accessibility

WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark: measured contrast, visible focus rings, targets of at least 24 × 24 px, nothing signalled by colour alone, reduced motion respected, every control usable from the keyboard, and single-key shortcuts that can be turned off.
