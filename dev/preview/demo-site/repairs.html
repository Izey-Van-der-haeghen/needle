Bike repairs and servicing in Riverside

Bring your bike to our workshop on Mill Lane and we will tell you what it needs before we start. Most punctures are fixed while you wait. A full service takes one working day.

Book a repair or call in without an appointment.

What does a service include?

  • Gears and brakes adjusted
  • Chain cleaned and lubricated
  • Wheels checked and trued
  • Safety check of frame, bolts and tyres
